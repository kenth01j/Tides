Reading the indicators

Compliance is based on bookings with an entry record. Revenue accuracy compares staff-approved paid booking totals with successful payment records. Platform adoption is an operational estimate using online visitors and recorded no-permit incidents. Visitor counts exclude cancelled bookings; revenue and payment-method totals include paid bookings even if later cancelled and do not account for refunds.

Visitors submit GCash or Maya payment receipt images for manual staff verification. No payment gateway or receiving account is connected to this site.
