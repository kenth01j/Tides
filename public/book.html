Kimud Shoal · Municipal Ordinance No. 2025-07

Visitor rules and penalties

Violations of the rules governing the Kimud Shoal Marine Protected Area and Aquatic/Eco-Tourism Site are subject to the following penalties.

Violations and penalties

OffensePenalty
First offense₱1,000 fine
Second offense₱1,500 fine
Third and succeeding offenses₱2,500 fine, imprisonment of up to six (6) months, or both, at the discretion of the court

Examples of prohibited activities

  • Fishing, coral destruction, or collecting marine life.
  • Touching, chasing, or disturbing thresher sharks.
  • Freediving, snorkeling, or other unauthorized underwater activities.
  • Conducting prohibited dive training activities.
  • Diving outside designated operating hours: 6:00 AM–2:00 PM.
  • Littering, pollution, or improper anchoring.
  • Violating diving safety and marine conservation protocols.

This summary is provided for visitor awareness. Please follow instructions from authorized local personnel.
